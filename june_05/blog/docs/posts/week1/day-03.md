# Week 1 — The Foundation

## Day 3

**Date:** June 3, 2026

**Title:** Working with LLMs — A New Kind of Tool

---

Day 3 shifted gears entirely. Instead of infrastructure and tooling, we started exploring Large Language Models — what they are, how they work at a high level, and more importantly, how to work *with* them effectively as a developer.

LLMs are not magic, and they're not a replacement for understanding. What they are is an incredibly capable collaborator — one that responds to how you communicate with it. The quality of your output depends heavily on the quality of your input. Vague prompts get vague answers. Specific, well-structured prompts get useful ones.

We talked about the difference between using an LLM as a search engine (bad) versus using it as a thought partner or rubber duck (good). The goal is to stay in the driver's seat — using AI to accelerate your thinking, not outsource it.

The key insight: **learn how to ask better questions**. That skill transfers everywhere.
