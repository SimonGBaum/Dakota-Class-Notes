# Francisco's Dev Journey

Welcome! This is my personal blog where I document the highs, the confusion, and the breakthroughs of becoming a Full-Stack Developer from scratch.

I'm currently enrolled in a full-stack software engineering program, and every week I'll be writing about what I learned, what broke, and what finally clicked. Think of it as a learning journal — raw, honest, and hopefully useful to anyone else on a similar path.

---

## What to Expect

Each post follows a simple format:

- **Date** — when it happened
- **Title** — what the day was about
- **Body** — my honest take on it

Posts are organized by week so you can follow along from the beginning or jump to whatever's relevant to you.
